# Missing original payloads

The recovered original `RoomzMain` loader references three original SWF payloads that are not yet recovered:

- `loadRoom.swf`
- `Intro.swf`
- `frame.swf`

These are currently the highest-priority missing pieces because the loader attempts to place them at Flash levels 107, 105, and 110 respectively.

Additional historical resources worth continuing to hunt:

- `numbs.html` (referenced by the RRR path)
- original Room 24 web dependency under `danger/map.htm`
- archived supporting HTML/resources from `theroomz.yoyo.pl`
- any room-content SWFs or modular packs loaded indirectly by `loadRoom.swf`

Do not substitute reconstructed rooms for missing originals in this preservation tree.
