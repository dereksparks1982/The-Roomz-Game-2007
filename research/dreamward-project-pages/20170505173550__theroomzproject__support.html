Support

The Roomz Project games are free-to-play: they run no adverts, have no commercial sponsorship, and are entirely dependant on donations to maintain.

Developing takes a lot of time and effort. Project is continuously expanding, new games and new levels are coming out successfully. If you, dear player, enjoy any of TRP games, find them entertaining, and would like to see new riddles in future - please, support the project. Your help is worth its weight in gold.

To support the project, click the button to the right. Thank you in advance for your kindness and generosity. If you happen to have any problems, send an e-mail to the address below. I'll respond as fast as possible.

Contact: the.roomz.project@gmail.com
