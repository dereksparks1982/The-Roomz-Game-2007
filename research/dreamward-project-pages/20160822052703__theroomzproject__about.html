About The Project

The Roomz Project is a series of interactive riddle games based on point-and-click genre, developed by DreamWard Games. Each of three games provides many hours of mind-stretching riddles and puzzles. Project has started in 2006 when the first riddle game, The Roomz, was made. After one year and a half all fifty roomz were completed and project has been closed. 3 years later The Roomz: Eclipse was born. In comparison to its precursor, the Eclipse was more polished, optimized and with a lot better graphics. In 2012 a new idea turned up. So far one could access a room only by solving the one before, and had to keep track of passwords to every level. This is the major difference between TR plus TRE and the newest game - The Roomz: Abandoned Pit. Password remembering is no more, due to the fact every room is open to public, no matter how many roomz player solved or not. TRAP is going to be released on December this year.

The Roomz

Status: finished (26 Dec 2007)
Content: 50 levels (including 5 bonuses)
Link: TheRoomz.org

The very first and very successful point-and-click riddle game. Aim of the game is to pass through forty five rooms filled with varied riddles with increasing difficulty. Each room contains a password to the next room, and is filled with hints to help with acquiring it. During the adventure player can use magnifying glass to zoom parts of the wall, scrolls and objects. There are many items to be found throughout the game.

The Roomz: Eclipse

Status: open (released 10 Oct 2010)
Content: 9 levels of 24 planned
Link: TheRoomzEclipse.org

A sequel which brings The Roomz features to the next level. Brand new graphics, refreshed layout, engine built from scratch. Although there are less levels, riddles are tougher and longer. It's not necessary to play The Roomz in order to enjoy exploring Eclipse. For those who seek for challenges, TRE includes many achievements to discover during adventure.

TR: Abandoned Pit

Status: open (released 21 Dec 2012)
Content: regularly-increasing levels
Link: DreamWardGames.com/trap/

Third game from The Roomz Project series. The biggest differences between TRAP and previous projects are: infinite number of riddles divided into 5 levels of difficulty - everyone can find something matching their abilities; no password remembering - each level is accessible from the start.
