 Welcome on THE ROOMZ ForOOm - the oficial forum of THE ROOMZ project. 
We were inspired by internet riddles, such as Zest Riddle and many others which you can find in the web. We didn't want to do next internet riddles, we wanted to do something different. 
The idea of THE ROOMZ project came to our minds in the middle of June. It was close to summer holidays so we had a lot more time than during school (yes, we are still pupils - almost students). 
We were walking through "Lazienki Park" (exactly here ) and we decided to do some riddles in FLASH. So we've done it. It wasn't easy job, but for sure it was a great fun. 
The best possibility we have is that we can make as many roomz as we want (it depends on our free time and ideas). 
This was really short story about the project. We hope you like it 
Here is a direct link to ROOMZ, if you forget it somehow 
 http://theroomz.yoyo.pl/ 
But what exactly is it? 
THE ROOMZ is the project done in Macromedia Flash 8. It's full of riddles and logical games so we think it is great for people who like to think 
The main objective is to pass through whole roomz. Each room's got its own plot and unique appearance. Levels of roomz are varied - from really easy to kinda tough and difficult. However, there's always logical way to solve it (at least we think so ). 
So if You've got a problem, write here in this forum - we'll try to help You
