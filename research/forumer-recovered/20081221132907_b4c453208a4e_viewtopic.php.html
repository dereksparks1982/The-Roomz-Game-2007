If you don't understand shorcuts we use, you can check their definitions here.

The Roomz shortcuts:
TR - The Roomz
MG - Magnifying Glass (in your inventory)
CTIF - Clearing Temporary Internet Files (when uploads are being made)
SB - Shout Box
MB - Message Board

Other:
10x - Thanks (with a special dedication to Ralfi)
btw - by the way
thx - thanks
lol - laugh out loud (for those that never played online games)
rotfl - rolling on the floor (stronger than "lol")
lmao - laugh my a** off (even stronger than "rotfl")
FAQ - Frequently asked questions (for those who never visited a forum)
Gratz - congratulations
GTG/G2G - got to go
k/kk - okay (or as some Australians say it O'l Korrect = O.K.)
L8er - later
ttyl - talk to you later
bb - bye bye
bbs - be back soon
wb - welcome back
brb - be right back
np - no problem
soz - sorry
sth - something
ftw - for the win
pwnz/ownz - ??? (sth like "rox"?)
rox - rocks (if sth is really good)
omg - oh my god
afaic - as far as I'm concerned
idk - I don't know
n/c - no comment
imo/imho - in my (honest) opinion
gg - good game
wtf/wth - what the f*** (hell)
atm - at the moment
jk - just kidding

Feel free to add some other shortcuts you don't understand or those which should be explained.
