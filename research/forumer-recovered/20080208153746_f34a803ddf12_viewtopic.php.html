Here are the rules about posting questions about rooms:

1) Use as much details as you can when describing your problem just to avoid misunderstandings.

2) DO NOT SPOIL the answers and DO NOT give too spoily hints

3) Telling the answer = breaking the rules. Such person will be BANNED and his/her IP will be blocked forever.

4) If you are going to create new topic for room which doesn't have its topic yet, keep the sheme: Room (number) - (short description). F.e.:
Room 1 - the first step

5) If the topic of a room already exists, DO NOT create another one. It will be immediately deleted without answering.

6) If you use a walkthrough, do not expect to be helped much on this forum. This forum is for small hints and nudges, NOT for cheating. If we believe you have cheated, you will receive no help from administrators or Appreciated Helpers.

7) Please do not double-post. Double-posting means posting two or more messages back-to-back in the same topic, with no-one else replying in between them. Instead, use the EDIT button, at the top-right of your post.

And of course use ENGLISH ONLY
