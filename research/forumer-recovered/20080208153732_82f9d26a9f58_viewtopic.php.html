From now on each roomer can rank each room's difficulty level.
The task is easy:
you create a table with 2 columns. 1st column is a room number, 2nd one is the difficult level of the room (matched from 1 to 5), where:
1- very easy
2 - easy
3 - moderate
4 - difficult
5 - very difficult
You can send it to me via PM or written in a notepad, MS Word or whatever.
Here is the example how to write it:
Code:
room - level
1 - 1
2 - 1
3 - 2
4 - 5
5 - 3
...
44 - 5

If u decided to give out your votes, rank at least 10 rooms. The best option for me would be ranking all available roomz but there are also roomers who didn't finish the game yet.
This ranking IS available to check here:
http://theroomz.yoyo.pl/rrr.htm

This ranking will show how people think each room is difficult. Please be sure u ranked each room correctly, there won't be any option to change it once u send it to me.

IMPORTANT: If u find it difficult to choose from i.e. *easy* or *moderate* level, you can write 2.5. But anyway try to avoid it.

Hope u'll like this idea. Waiting for your votes
