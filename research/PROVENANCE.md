## Historical evidence recovered

Archived `roomz.htm` identifies Stachu & Ralfi and describes the game as having **over thirty levels**. The original loader proves a modular architecture and explicitly requests `loadRoom.swf`, `Intro.swf`, and `frame.swf`.

Historical comments/password records show progress well beyond the surviving three-room demo, including rooms in the 30s. Those records are evidence, not recovered executable content.
